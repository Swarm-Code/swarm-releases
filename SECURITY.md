# Security policy

Please do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public issues or
discussions.

Report security concerns privately to `security@swarmcode.ai` and include:

- the affected SwarmOS version and platform;
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept;
- expected impact;
- any suggested mitigation.

We will acknowledge receipt, investigate, coordinate remediation, and publish
an advisory when appropriate. Do not include credentials, customer data, or
other sensitive information beyond what is necessary to reproduce the issue.
